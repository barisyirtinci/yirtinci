How it works

Two systems, not a step count.

Human alertness is driven by two processes that have been measured for forty years. Zone models both, then subtracts one from the other. That is the whole trick — and it is why the forecast holds up without a wearable strapped to you.

Sleep pressure

A chemical debt that builds from the moment you wake and only clears while you sleep. Miss an hour and it carries into tomorrow.

Body clock

A 24-hour rhythm anchored to your temperature minimum. It does not care that you are tired — it lifts you in the evening whether you wanted that or not.

What you actually feel

Morning peak, afternoon dip, second wind, and the drop that follows. Not a mood — an arithmetic result.

Your day

Named to the minute, not to the afternoon.

Zone reads the turning points out of your own curve. Not “most people dip after lunch” — your dip, at the time it actually starts.
