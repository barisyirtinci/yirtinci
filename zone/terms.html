2. What the app is

Zone models human alertness from published chronobiology — Borbély's two-process model and the Åkerstedt/Folkard three-process formulation — using the sleep you log. It produces a forecast, not a measurement.

A forecast is an estimate. It is built from a general model and your own entries, and it can be wrong — particularly if what you log is inaccurate, if you work shifts, travel across time zones, take medication that affects sleep, or have an undiagnosed sleep disorder. Do not rely on Zone for any decision where being wrong would be dangerous. It is your responsibility to judge whether you are fit to drive, operate machinery, or perform safety-critical work, and no output of this app changes that.
