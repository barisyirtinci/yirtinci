Approach

Three rules, applied without exception.

Model, don't decorate

If a number appears on screen it was computed from something, and the something is published work rather than a rule of thumb. A guess with a progress ring around it is still a guess.

Your data stays yours

No account, no server, no analytics or advertising SDK. Not a policy promise — there is nowhere for it to go. Export it to a file whenever you like.

Never hold the user hostage

Everything you record, and everything derived from it, stays free. A subscription buys new capability, never access to your own history.
