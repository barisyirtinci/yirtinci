Approach

Three rules, whatever the product.

Make the real thing

If Zone shows a number, it was computed from published science. If a tile in Lantern Bay has a cat on it, someone drew that cat. No stock templates, no filler, nothing that only looks finished.

Say exactly what happens to your data

Each product has its own privacy page listing everything it stores and every service it talks to. Zone talks to none: no server, no ads, no analytics. Lantern Bay is a free game with ads and an online league, and its page says precisely what that involves.

Never hold anyone hostage

What you record or earn stays yours. Paying buys something new or takes something away — new features, no ads — never access to your own history or progress.
