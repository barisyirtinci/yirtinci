Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Lantern Bay mobile game on iOS and Android

The game itself runs on your phone, and your progress is stored there. A free game also needs a few outside services — ads, store purchases and the optional weekly league — and this page lists every one of them, what it receives and why. You do not need to create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Who this is from

Lantern Bay is made by Barış Yırtıncı, trading as yirtinci, an independent one-person developer, who is the data controller for the data described here. Questions about this policy go to support@yirtinci.com.

Stored on your phone

Your level, coins, stars, boosters, lives, town progress, collected items and settings (language, sound, music, vibration) are saved in the app's own storage on your device, with a backup copy so an interrupted save cannot erase your progress. This data is removed when you delete the game.

Sent to outside services

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Google AdMob Device and app information, IP address, ad interactions, and — only with your permission — the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) To show ads that keep the game free, measure them and prevent ad fraud
Apple App Store / Google Play Your purchase, under your store account To process in-app purchases. We never see your card details, name or email.
RevenueCat An anonymous identifier for this install and the store receipt To confirm purchases and restore them on a new phone
Google Firebase — anonymous sign-in and App Check A random player ID created by the app, and a check that the request comes from the genuine app So league scores and saved progress belong to the right player and cannot be faked
Google Firebase — weekly league and cloud save The display name you choose, your cat's outfit, your league scores and the moves of the levels you submit; a copy of your game progress To rank players fairly (scores are checked by replaying the moves) and to restore progress on a new device
Google Firebase — Analytics and Crashlytics Anonymous gameplay events (for example, a level started or won), device model, OS version and crash reports To find crashes and to balance levels that are too hard or too easy

These providers process data under their own policies: Google (AdMob and Firebase), RevenueCat, Apple. Some of them process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in those policies.

Ads, tracking and your choices

  • On iOS, the game asks for permission before the advertising identifier is used. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using that identifier.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google appears before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the game's settings.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • A No Ads purchase turns off the ads that play between levels.

What the game does not do

  • No account creation, and no name, email address or phone number collected.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • The weekly league is optional; only the display name you choose is visible to other players.

How long data is kept

League scores are kept for the week they belong to and a short time afterwards, then deleted. A cloud copy of your progress is kept while you play and deleted on request or after a long period of inactivity. Advertising, purchase and analytics providers keep data for the periods set out in their own policies.

Children

Lantern Bay is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the league or cloud save, write to us and we will delete the data.

Your rights and deleting your data

Under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and Turkey's KVKK you can ask for access to, correction of, a copy of or erasure of personal data we hold about you, and you can object to processing. To delete your league entry and cloud save, write to support@yirtinci.com with the display name you use in the league; we answer within 30 days. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your phone. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts doing something this page does not describe, this page changes first and the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will also be shown in the game.
