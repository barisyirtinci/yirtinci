How it plays

Easy to learn, hard to put down.

Tap a free tile

Tiles are stacked in layers. Only the ones on top can move, so look before you tap.

✦

Match three

Three of a kind in your tray vanish with a pop. Chain matches together for a combo.

Keep the tray clear

The tray only holds a few tiles. Fill it up and the round is over — plan a move or two ahead.

Your town

Five places to bring back to life.

Stars from every level rebuild cottages, piers and lighthouses. Finish a place and the next one opens up.

Every day

Always something to come back to.

Daily Puzzle

One new board a day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going.

Lantern Climb

A weekly event: climb to the top of the lighthouse, stage by stage, for bigger rewards.

Weekly League

See how your week compares with other players. Scores are checked, so the board stays fair.

Quests & trophies

Small daily goals, a trophy room to fill and a postcard album from every place you rebuild.

Miso’s wardrobe

Dress your cat companion up, and give your tiles a new look to match your mood.

Seasons

The bay changes with the calendar, with seasonal tiles around the holidays.

Fair play

Free to play, without the tricks.

Every board can be cleared

No level is built to be impossible. If you lose, a better plan wins next time.

No ad after a loss

Ads help keep the game free, but they never interrupt you right after you lose a round.

One purchase removes forced ads

Optional purchases are coins and extras. A single No Ads purchase turns off the ads that play between levels.

Your progress is safe

Progress is saved on your phone as you play and survives updates. Nothing you earned is ever taken back.
